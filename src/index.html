How to turn rolls into a seed

Dice rolls (faces 1–6 only)

Base 10 [0-9] hashes the original roll string, matching COLDCARD and SeedSigner. Dice [1-6] converts each 6 to 0 before hashing, matching the method used by Keystone.

Read a PSBT. Check its ECDSA nonces.

      
Inspecting a PSBT v0 does not require a private key. EntropyLab can show outputs, PSBT-provided input amounts and fees, signatures, and repeated ECDSA nonce values. Loading a matching key additionally checks whether supported signatures match plain RFC 6979; a mismatch alone is not evidence of a compromised signer.
